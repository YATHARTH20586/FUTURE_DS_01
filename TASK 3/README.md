# Task 3 – Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## Business question
Where do users drop off between visitor, lead, trial and customer, and which channels perform best?

## Data
- Marketing funnel and lead data (`Excel Data for Power BI – Raw Data`), cleaned before analysis.

## Tool
- **Power BI** – data modelling and interactive dashboard (`Marketing_Funnel_Analysis_2026.pbix`)

## Dashboard
- Funnel chart by stage (Visitor, Lead, Trial, Customer)
- Total reach over time (Jan–Mar 2026)
- Total reach by channel: Facebook Ads, Organic Search, Referral, Google Ads, Email
- Channel filter and a total reach KPI of **250,000**

## Key insights
- **The funnel narrows sharply.** About 175K visitors became 67K leads (roughly 38%), then 7K trials (about 4% of visitors, 10% of leads), and only about 0.1% of visitors ended up as customers.
- **The biggest drop is at the end of the funnel.** About 9 in 10 leads never reach a trial, and almost all trials fail to become customers. Fixing the last two stages offers the biggest gain.
- **Reach is spread evenly across channels.** Facebook Ads, Organic Search, Referral, Google Ads and Email bring in almost the same total reach, so no single channel dominates. Reach alone cannot show which channel is best; conversion by channel is needed.

## Recommendations
1. Focus first on the trial-to-customer step: onboarding, follow-up emails, and a clear offer at the end of the trial.
2. Improve lead-to-trial conversion with a shorter sign-up and quicker follow-up after a lead arrives.
3. Track conversion rate per channel, not just reach, and move budget to the channel that converts best.
4. Review the funnel monthly to catch drops early.
